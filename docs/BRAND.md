# Veyra

A made-up name for the moment an object becomes a favorite. Pronounced “VAY-rah”. Naming is a creative proposal, not trademark clearance.

**Voice:** quiet anticipation, personal discovery, clear control. Tagline: “A little luck. A rare feeling.”

**Visual system:** ink #111114, archive panel #19191e, warm paper #f2efe8, foil violet #c4afff, policy mint #bfdfcb. Inter variable typography, small monospace catalog details, restrained corners, editorial scale and generous negative space. No decorative terminal walls on the main screen.

**Mark:** a split circular seal. The two halves suggest a pack opening and a collectible being revealed. The same shape forms the foil orbit, transition device and film end card.

**Packs:** First light (sage), Afterglow (violet), Fireline (copper). Procedural CSS foil, crimped edges, layered orbit engraving, quiet microtype. No invented Pokémon illustrations.

**Motion:** move from broad context to the action, maintain a clear reading order, hold the reveal, and return to the mark. Interface motion uses Framer Motion. Video motion is deterministic and frame-driven in Remotion; browser animation clocks are disabled in rendered scenes.

**References studied:** Ordinary Folk's process and portfolio; official Remotion skills, transition and audio documentation. These inform the use of storyboards, staged hierarchy, continuous object motifs and beat timing; the composition is original.
